About the Artist

A voice shaped by home

12+Years performing
30+Released songs
Folk / ModernSignature sound

Pavel is a Bangladeshi singer-songwriter known for blending the melodic phrasing of traditional Bengali folk with the textures of modern production. Raised around the sound of harmonium and tabla, he began writing songs in his teens and has spent the years since refining a style that feels both rooted and current.

His live shows move between stripped-back acoustic sets and full-band arrangements, and his recordings favor warm, organic instrumentation over heavy processing. Pavel has performed at festivals and venues across the country and continues to release new music independently, writing every song from the same simple idea: that music should feel like it's speaking directly to the listener.
